## Highest-impact next changes

1. **Make the heading change follow a readable support exchange.** Establish the accepting leg, unload the other foot, then pivot or place it into the new direction before transferring weight. A pivot can be small, but its heel/toe release and support point need to read clearly. Preserve the useful small placements while reducing the long stretches in which both boots appear to turn with the whole body.

2. **Stage the body through that exchange.** Give the pelvis a modest arrival over the support leg, let the chest and carried rifle follow, and finish with a small settle. Keep the current continuous grip and carry path. The missing element is coordinated weight and timing, not extra head gestures or a larger decorative sway.
