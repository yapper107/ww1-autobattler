# Pass 56 — continued standing rifle turn

26 September 2026. Bounded independent rendered-frame comparison with pass 54.

**Provisional turn-only grades: female 6.0/10; male 6.0/10.** There is a modest visible improvement over pass 54's 5.5/10 on each body: a lifted trailing foot now follows a more readable path around the supporting leg. The turn still lacks convincing coordination between that exchange and the body's rotation and final weight arrival. It has not reached the user's **8.5/10 AAA/TLOU2-level** anchor. The rejected original remains the **3/10** anchor.

This does not establish a new whole-system grade, nor approve any unrecaptured part of the course.

## Visible result

**The foot sequence is more differentiated.** In frames **30–41 (7.5–7.867 seconds)**, one boot lifts and passes behind the supporting leg while the nearer boot remains more consistently oriented. The character no longer spends the entire middle of the turn in the earlier broadly open stance. This is a useful improvement in the visible explanation of the turn: [male native sequence](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/male-turn-a-native.jpg), [female native sequence](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/female-turn-a-native.jpg).

At **frame 42 (7.9 seconds)** and **frame 48 (8.1 seconds)**, the candidate passes through a narrower support configuration instead of pass 54's open, planted-looking pivot. The moving boot follows behind and then begins to emerge into the new orientation. Compare [male frame 42](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/male-54vs56-0042.jpg), [female frame 42](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/female-54vs56-0042.jpg), [male frame 48](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/male-54vs56-0048.jpg), [female frame 48](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/female-54vs56-0048.jpg). **The body does not yet make the exchange feel fully weighted.** The chest and rifle continue to change heading fairly evenly while the feet perform the more distinct action below. Pelvis movement and knee loading remain restrained. The new leg path is clearer than the resulting sense of mass moving onto the accepting support. I cannot identify the implementation cause from these images.

**The finish remains weakly resolved.** Around **frames 54–74 (8.3–8.967 seconds)**, the stance is narrow and the trailing side remains visibly toe-supported, with little clearly staged body settling. A raised heel is not inherently wrong, and I do not require both soles to lie flat. The limitation is that the pose does not strongly communicate that weight has arrived and the turn is complete before the next foot action. See [male exit crops](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/male-exit-native.jpg), [female exit crops](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/female-exit-native.jpg), and the matched **8.5-second** poses: [male](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/male-54vs56-0060.jpg), [female](/home/jchan/.codex/worktrees/gasp-soldier-animation/ww1-autobattler/.local/animation-review/pass56-continued-turn/female-54vs56-0060.jpg).

## Highest-impact next changes

1. **Coordinate the body's change of heading with the visible support exchange.** Preserve the useful free-foot path, but make rotation and pelvis travel feel supported by the planted leg and the subsequent placement. The improvement needed is the relationship between body motion and contact timing, not merely a larger step.

2. **Resolve the final weight arrival.** Give the accepting leg a readable loading response and let the pelvis/chest settle into a deliberate ready stance before the next action. Preserve the continuous rifle grip and carriage. Adding extra sway would not address the remaining problem.
