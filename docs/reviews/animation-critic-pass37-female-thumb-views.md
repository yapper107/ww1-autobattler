## Remaining performance work

The clearest remaining quality limitation is the feed's staging at **60–76**: the raised operating forearm dominates the silhouette, then lowers with the hand over the receiver. There is a small body/rifle settle, but relatively little distinct shoulder, trunk or support-hand response to distinguish alignment, applied pressure and release. This is a qualitative weight/overlap criticism, not a contact error or a claim that the body is motionless. The open-hand transition **76–81** is connected but still reads as a deliberate pose change more than relaxed follow-through.

The next useful polish would strengthen that small force-and-release response while preserving contact and the repaired arm path. Pickup pinch and bolt regrip deserve verification in a useful close view before prescribing a geometry change; do not enlarge gestures merely to defeat ordinary occlusion. Continuous playback at the intended gameplay framing remains necessary for timing acceptance. These focused views do not establish locomotion, male/MG quality or full-course transitions.
