## Verdict

No hidden-state reads in the new code. `UpdateCognitivePlan`, `CognitiveOrders`, `RememberedRisk`, `ReceivedUsefulSupport`, and the cognition attention branch read only the leader's memory, own order, received receipts, and friendly positions. `detectionDelay` is computed inside `SenseEnemy` from the sample that passed (`BattleSim.cpp:570`) and only shifts reaction time (`ReactionSim.cpp:16`). The digest covers the accepted record and profile (`Diagnostics.cpp:108-112`). The gaps below are transition semantics and tests that pass for the wrong reason.

**1. Blocked is reported upward before local alternatives are exhausted.** The squad report maps `stage==Blocked` straight to Blocked (`PlatoonSim.cpp:160-162`), but the plan only decides whether it still has a local method on the next plan tick (`CognitiveSim.cpp:99-105`, `:151`). Reports run every 3 s, plans every 1 to 8 s, so a Blocked SupportedAdvance usually reaches the platoon while the squad is about to accept AlternateApproach. The platoon then issues Observe and overrides the squad's own alternative. Scenario 10 passes because of this race, not because of exhaustion, and its predicates cannot tell the difference (`cognition_tests.h:91-100`). Remedy: set an `exhausted` flag at the two early returns and at the "no eligible moving candidate" return, report Blocked only when it is set, and assert in the scenario that no `method_accepted` for the same parent follows the Blocked `goal_feedback_sent`.

**2. AlternateApproach accepts destinations with no relationship to the objective.** The comment at `CognitiveSim.cpp:135` promises an observation or firing relationship, but the filter is only distance under 30 m from the objective and 8 m from the failed point (`:137-138`). In scenario 10 the cover positions on the near face of the new wall qualify, the squad walks to the wall, latches Complete with "enemy control unverified" (`:97`), and reports Done for the parent goal. Remedy: require a clear line from the candidate peek to the sector, or the candidate inside `intent.radius` of the objective. With that filter scenario 10 exhausts honestly and finding 1 becomes testable.

**3. Terminal states are absorbing, and Prepare can loop forever.** Complete returns unconditionally (`:103`); Blocked Observe, AlternateApproach, and Withdraw return unconditionally (`:105`). Prepare timeout transitions to Blocked without recording the method as failed (`:90`), so the next tick re-accepts SupportedAdvance and waits another 40 s. Expiry transitions to Blocked and re-proposes with the expired parent and a fresh 60 s intent (`:62`, `:153-155`), which contradicts the results doc claim that expired goals stop tagging tasks. On layouts 5 to 7 the maneuver squad will complete one route and idle until the platoon refreshes the directive near expiry, roughly 48 s. Remedy: after `reconsiderAt` in Complete or exhausted Blocked, re-propose with parent zero and the mission objective. Prepare timeout adds SupportedAdvance to `failedMethods`. Expiry sets parent zero.

**4. The support gate accepts anyone's fire, and nothing establishes support.** `ReceivedUsefulSupport` filters only `shooter<0` (`:12-20`). Delivery reports from movers holding in Prepare, and from the leader, all relay to the leader and satisfy it. `CognitiveOrders` gives the gun Overwatch at wherever it stood at acceptance (`:170-174`), so a gun without a line to the sector never fires and Prepare times out. The method neither selects a support position nor verifies the support element. The test seeds shooter 7 and cannot discriminate (`cognition_tests.h:10-13`). Remedy: require `shooter==command.support` or a machine gunner, and in Assess choose a gun position with the existing PlanSquad support loop (protected from sector, clear line, reachable). Add the mirrored test where only a mover fires and assert Prepare does not release.

**5. Reconsideration fires on ageing, not new threats.** `knowledgeRevision` changes on nearly every receipt, and `RememberedRisk` grows with `TrackUncertainty` (`:21-27`), so any advance toward a known contact exceeds 0.5 once `committedUntil` passes. The trace reason claims a new threat (`:60`). The adaptability test passes only because the slow profile is time-gated (`cognition_tests.h:115-122`). Remedy: store risk at commit and require a contact observed after `p.started` inside the destination radius.

**6. One reloading mover pauses the whole squad.** Any Interrupted receipt sets the override (`:69-75`), including cause Reload, which every rifleman emits for 2.5 s each eight rounds. Each pause pushes `reconsiderAt` by 20 s, so the movement timeout rarely fires, stage advance waits for a quiet moment, and traces spam. Remedy: count only Shelter and Passage causes and require at least half the movers or the point element.

**Minor.**
- The officer profile is one config value copied to every soldier on both teams (`BattleSim.cpp:168`). Do not describe it as per-actor seeding.
- Scenario 9 aborts if the commander ever sees the enemy rather than measuring it (`cognition_tests.h:128-129`). Staff follow the squad anchor and can walk into range on longer runs.
- Evaluator compatibility: `proposal_accepted` is emitted only when a route exists (`:165`) and `plan_completed` at `:97`. Diff the populated trace fields against a legacy run of layout 5 once before the matrix, since the evaluator may read fields the cognition entries leave empty.

Fix 1 through 4 before running the 5 to 7 matrix. Findings 5 and 6 change behavior but not correctness of the feature claims.
