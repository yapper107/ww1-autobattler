## Accepted sequence

1. Retain physically executable routes. A cheaper path is insufficient reason to replace a coordinated plan. Permit a repair when a required mover cannot execute the retained continuation and the proposed path is executable. Preserve mission, deadlines and requested/refused support facts. Measure original nine in isolation before the remaining changes.
2. Record failed support with the actual shooter, threat and focus. Old-sector fire cannot clear a refusal. Reject another approach to the same refused final threat until actual relevant fire or a materially different support/geometry arrangement makes it feasible.
3. Retain exhausted status long enough for the real round-trip failure report and replacement order. Small enemy movement cannot erase failure before the platoon hears it. A materially revised directive can act immediately; an equivalent order copy cannot reset failed work or deadlines.
4. Higher command avoids freshly known occupied viewpoints and reported failed objectives. Observation has a bounded follow-up; it cannot become a repeating substitute for a useful mission revision. With no alternative, hold/withdraw honestly.
5. Local alternate viewpoints require protected shelter against the received threat set. A view of the enemy alone is insufficient.

## Implementation resolutions

- Fable's path-refusal exception requires a different, unrefused final threat set. A path-only change to an unchanged destination cannot establish that fact. Step1 therefore enables physical path repair only; changing the objective belongs to the platoon after real failure feedback.
- Append support identity/focus metadata with safe defaults to failure records. Transport preserves original failure time. Include new state in diagnostics, digest and replay restoration.
- An equivalent order serial is not a materially new directive and cannot erase persistent failed-approach evidence. This preserves the user's earlier contract while permitting genuinely revised missions immediately.
- Existing authored tactical cases, evaluator, weapon parameters and360s gate remain unchanged. Mechanism fixtures may add controlled geometry and durable targets, but all positive integration assertions require production sensing, navigation, execution and transport.

## Discriminating evidence

- Cheaper but executable continuation retains its accepted route; physically blocked corridor admits an executable repair. Trace parity and actual geometry mutation coverage remain required.
- Gun continues old-sector fire after refusal: same final dependency remains ineligible. Two genuinely delivered rounds at the refused focus permit reconsideration.
- Enemy movement changes its knowledge bucket during escalation: Blocked reaches the platoon; the corresponding changed mission is received before the bounded wait expires.
- Commander rejects a fresh enemy-occupied objective and a failed support objective, with no-feedback/cleared-evidence controls.
- Exposed alternate viewpoints are rejected; a protected reachable control can execute.
