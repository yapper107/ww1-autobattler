**1. Is it a successful bootstrap?** As an engineering bootstrap, yes: the model reproduces the teacher closely enough that the attack score is level on 105 paired battles, clears 44 of 45 unseen validation cities, replays bit-exactly on two platforms, and passes every attack conduct guard. What the tests establish is narrower than "works on new scenarios": the validation cities are new maps of the same mission family (twelve static defenders, three known layouts, fixed roster, attack only). Both intervals straddle zero, so there is no evidence of improvement or of harm on that family. Nothing is established for other mission families: the symmetric city and trench sets were smoke tests, and on them the model fails the Azure order-rate guard and loses more men in trenches (unpaired, ten battles, no interval). One training seed means the variance of the training procedure itself is unknown. The demonstration set also has 18 presses and 40 withdrawals among 20,612 rows, so those families are effectively unlearned; the executor's gates and the candidate restrictions are doing more of the work than the network. Jordan's reading is right: the imitation did its job, which was to prove the pipe, not to improve the squad.

**2. The left-behind men.** The city 28 snapshot shows five blue men strung along a wall well north of their corporal while legacy's men are grouped on the leader. That is consistent with at least four mechanisms, and a snapshot cannot separate them: the covering pair (legitimate, at most two men, released within 10 s); men dispatched to bound stations of a plan the ranker then replaced, since a new target more than 10 m away restarts the preparation clock and re-lays stations; an exposed candidate chosen without covering fire, so the squad sits in the 20 s support wait with men already moving; or a shattered squad attached as support one objective behind by plan 023's ruling. The first is by design, the last is by ruling, and only the middle two are the ranker's fault. The bounded prerequisite is cheap: re-run the 60 development pairs with exports kept and report `behind_corporal_share`, `straggler_share`, `replaced_before_arrival` and regroup orders paired against legacy, then trace city 28 seed 108 squad by squad with the decision outcome log (retain, support wait, preparing, committed). Those measures exist and were not in the pilot table. This can run alongside RL plumbing, but it must land before the reward is fixed, because if the cause is churn or support waits the fix is the commitment interface, not a distance term. Any distance penalty would punish the covering pair and the attached remnant.

**3. Reward and PPO now?** Build the plumbing now; do not start a training run until the commitment interface exists. The churn guard failure is the reason: the actor re-selects every 1 to 2 s, "action zero" is a hold rather than a keep, and PPO exploration will amplify exactly that.

Proposed initial reward, weights are proposals: per squad decision interval, a shared team reward r = ΔD/12 − 0.5·ΔA/32, where ΔD is defenders newly out of action and ΔA is own attackers newly lost over the interval, both taken from training truth in the Python trainer, never from the actor's features. The undiscounted episode return then equals the loop's attack score, so the reward optimises what the guards and the loop already rank. No time term at first: the objective has none, and a per-second cost buys speed with losses; measure duration instead. Terminal handling follows Astra's resolution 3: the 600 s deadline is terminal, no bootstrap; a shorter collection cutoff is truncation with a bootstrapped value. Discount by elapsed seconds with a rate chosen so the horizon covers a bound and an assault, about 120 s, and state the resulting half-life. Team credit: every squad receives the same team reward for its own interval, and a centralised critic reads privileged truth (true enemy and defender state, all friendly squads) through a separate typed path; the actor keeps schema 2 and the static check. Do not add shots, motion, flank labels or proximity: the casualty terms already reward closing where closing kills.

The limitation to state plainly: this reward is the static-defence attack score. A policy trained on it learns attack against men who never move, on one roster, and is judged for defence, symmetric fights and trenches only by smoke tests and the churn guard. Broadening the objective is a separate design decision for Jordan, not a weight change.

**4. Minimal correct next slice.** (a) A `keep` action distinct from hold that retains the prepared target and clock, with the executor's plan identity preserved. (b) A persistent lean worker mode in the lab: successive battles per process, decisions over a pipe, policy RNG separate from combat RNG, per-squad transitions with observation, mask, action, log-probability, elapsed time, reward and terminal flag. (c) A masked PPO trainer whose actor is the same scorer initialised from the imitation weights, a critic over privileged summaries, elapsed-time GAE, and a KL penalty to the frozen imitation policy. (d) Evaluation as now plus the frozen imitation model as a third paired arm, at least three training seeds, and a validation draw keyed to the experiment rather than the model hash, so checkpoints can be compared on one draw and a final unopened draw remains. (e) The four cohesion measures added to the neural report as guards. Churn is a guard against shipping, and a hard stop for a training run until (a) exists. Capacity is not the limit at 3,137 parameters; authority is, and widening candidates (mode 30) should stay a separate experiment after (a), since more candidates without keep semantics means more ways to churn.
