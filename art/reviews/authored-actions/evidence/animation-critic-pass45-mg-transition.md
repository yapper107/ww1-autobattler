## Remaining priorities

1. **Keep the shoulder shell over the shoulder instead of rolling it with the arm.** High frames **49–55 (31.133–31.333 s)** show the operating plate turning until its cup/underside faces toward the neck and the upper arm becomes uncovered. The abnormal position persists during the pouch reach/hold and recurs around feeding **96–104** and charging **121–124**. `high-shoulder.jpg`, `high-feed.jpg` and `high-charge.jpg` show this directly. It is a visible equipment-attachment failure, even though its rotation is continuous. The forthcoming armor change is not present here and receives no credit.

2. **Make the body and supporting arm communicate the work.** The torso now participates in retrieval and the gun dips and recovers around seating/pressing **89–104** and closure **112–118**. Those responses exist; they should not be described as a frozen body. Nevertheless the gestures still read as a series of carefully positioned arm operations, with modest differentiation between carrying the replacement, seating it, pressing the feed and charging. Author clearer anticipation, brief resistance and release through the supporting side while preserving the repaired contacts. Increasing all motion amplitudes would not by itself solve this.

3. **Give the carried equipment a restrained physical response.** The widened pouch provides necessary clearance, but its mouth and walls remain rigid through the hand/box exchange. It follows the hips with little independent settling during recovery and movement. This is a lower-priority motion issue than the rotating armor; it is not an objection to the low-poly visual style or a demand for a specific cloth implementation.

Cover opening, feed and charging remain coherent across the two views. The high view shows the glove alongside the cover, fingers over the feed region and the rearward charging gesture. Some opposing contact surfaces remain hidden; I did not establish a floating-hand gap from that occlusion.

Repair the visible armor behavior first, then refine load response and equipment settling. Recheck both views and actual continuous playback afterward.
