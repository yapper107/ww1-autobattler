The far supporting hand and stock-to-body contact remain largely hidden, so their exact pressure/clearance cannot be certified.

It is a secondary polish concern; playback should decide whether restrained counterbalance or timing changes are needed. The four-second duration itself is not a defect, and I would not add arbitrary pauses or exaggerated body motion from these stills alone.
