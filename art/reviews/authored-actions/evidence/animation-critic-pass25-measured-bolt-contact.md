The surrounding frames show a route, but the reversal does not read as one comfortable, economical transport gesture. Fix that route before polishing fingers.

2. **The feeding pose obscures pressure and release.** Approximately **80–107 (30.917–31.817)** keeps the forearm high across the torso with the large cuff above the receiver and the glove folded steeply down beneath it. Native front and side **84** and **105** are useful correction targets. Compared with pass 24, a complete arm remains visible, but the contact silhouette is less legible. Gameplay **59–76 (30.217–30.783)** retains the same folded pose. Reorient the wrist and lower/open the forearm enough to show grasp, thumb pressure and an unmistakably empty release without creating a new shoulder loop.

3. **Pouch access remains visually crowded.** The lower elbow makes opening more credible, but the stock crosses the pouch-mouth region at **51–69 (29.950–30.550)**. Native front **54** shows the glove, flap/rim and wooden stock overlapping in projection; the side view also fails to reveal an unambiguous fingertip pinch. This is a contact-readability and clearance target, not a claim that every overlap proves penetration. Give the flap grip and extraction a clear silhouette in both views. The raised green rim against the stock remains conspicuous during feeding, including front **84**.
