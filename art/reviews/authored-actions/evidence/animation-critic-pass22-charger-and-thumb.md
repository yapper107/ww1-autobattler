Pass 21's equivalent frames did not contain this protruding stack. The previously successful seating result therefore cannot be carried forward as passing.

3. **A credible flap grip and extraction remain unresolved.** The exact green stripe across the sleeve at pass 21's **49–50** is absent. That is a narrow clearance gain. The strip becomes visible during 73–76, but its association with a hand folded against the forearm is a new contact/readability failure. A visible object and an open bag are not sufficient evidence of successful retrieval.

No new grade is assigned to the sampled shot/bolt portion, and interruption continuity was not exhaustively rechecked in this pass.
