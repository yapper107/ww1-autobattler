Retrieval has body participation, but docking **84–90**, feed settling **96–104**, and cover closure **112–117** still rely heavily on the operating arm changing pose while the weapon and supporting side remain relatively steady. Add small, timed counter-bracing and recovery at those events rather than a general body bob. Judge that change in actual-speed playback before increasing its amplitude.

The second priority is verification of the **actual grasp surfaces** at opening 28–35, feeding 96–104 and charging 120–125 from a close opposite/high view. This is a coverage requirement, not a claim that ordinary occlusion is bad animation. Preserve the comfortable wrist and corrected pouch route unless another view demonstrates a fault.
